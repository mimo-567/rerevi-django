Build your paper

Arrange topic and question-type blocks inside numbered containers. For example, use A · Christianity or D · Life and Death. ReRevi picks the actual questions when you press Build paper.

Paper settings

Start from a layout

Full component layouts include SPaG only where it is assessed. Topic layouts omit SPaG by default.

Arrange your blocks

Numbered containers hold your question blocks. Drag blocks into place or use the Add and move controls. These are question choices, not exact questions.

Block palette

Drag onto the canvas or click to add.

Paper canvas

Move whole containers by their handles. Drop a question-type block on another block to insert it above, or in the space at the bottom to add it last. Your layout stays in this browser tab.

Choose the actual questions

Build paper selects matching questions for your blocks. Rebuild to choose alternatives where available. Repeated wording is avoided when possible. Empty containers are not printed.
