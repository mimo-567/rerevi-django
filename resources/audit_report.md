# Route A resource audit — 12 October 2026

## Corrections applied

- SPaG eligibility: Component 1 question 1(d) and Component 2 Christianity question 1(d), each up to 6 additional marks. None in Component 3 Islam. Practice scoring remains optional and off by default. Corrected the earlier omission of Christianity.
- Component 1 C questions have their own exact band descriptors, explicitly assessing diversity. Responses need two religions or two religious traditions; Humanism does not satisfy that requirement.
- Component 1 Life and Death 2(d) uses its own D descriptors and requires non-religious beliefs alongside religious beliefs. Humanists and Atheists are examples; the instruction does not require a separate account of both. This requirement does not apply automatically to every Life and Death part.
- Shared bands alone are insufficient for self marking. Attached each of the 192 archived B/C/D parts to its matching original indicative content, source scheme URL and PDF page. Indicative content is not an exhaustive checklist.
- Restored question words lost by the previous importer and removed extraction control characters. Historical prompts remain stored; a separate practice prompt uses current D instructions.
- Replaced paraphrased glossary definitions with source wording, retaining the original combined entries for provenance. There are 48 official glossary entries across the six selected groups, with 51 selectable terms when good/evil, absolute/relative poverty and greater/lesser jihad are split.
- A questions remain outside the archived B/C/D bank. Christianity and Islam each share their own keyword pool across beliefs and practices. The fixed keyword stem is a practice template; actual papers sometimes use religion-specific wording.
- A marking accepts equivalent accurate wording, not exact text matches. An example is not compulsory. Own words can receive full marks; an example alone or an accurate one-word response can receive one mark according to the latest guidance. The site should show the scheme for human self marking.
- D questions require more than one viewpoint; the viewpoints do not have to contrast. Do not force a for/against answer structure.

## Before public launch

WJEC's publication policy allows acknowledged classroom and private-study use, but says written permission must be requested for reproduction outside centres, including electronic copies. A public website reproducing questions, schemes or paper layouts needs that permission. Public download availability is not a public-republication licence. No permission has been obtained or application sent during this audit.

The print view should identify the output as an unofficial practice paper. Use source references and preserve each historical question's provenance. This audit updates the resource data; it does not implement the website or its PDF/email flows.
